Optional usage counts

Updated 1 October 2026. Rory Bain operates roryba.in and photos.roryba.in. For privacy questions or rights requests, contact rorydbain@gmail.com.

Your choice

Usage counting is off by default. If you choose Allow counts, this site sends a public page path to a small service I operate to help me understand which pages are useful and improve the site. You can decline without losing access to any content. Select Stop counting in the controls at the bottom of any page to withdraw at any time, as easily as you gave permission. Consent is the basis for optional counting; withdrawing does not affect processing already carried out.

The choice applies only to this site's browser origin, not to the other site or your other browsers. Your browser remembers the choice and its date for up to 180 days in local storage, solely to honour your preference. It is not a visitor identifier and is never sent to the server. Clear site storage to reset it. If your browser cannot save the preference, counting stays off. Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control signals also switch counting off, even if you previously allowed it.

What the counter keeps

The counter stores daily totals by site and public page path for up to 90 days. Before deleting older daily counts, it rolls their site totals into calendar-month totals. Monthly totals contain only a site, month and number of views, with no page paths or visitor information; these are kept indefinitely so I can compare usage over many years. Query strings, fragments and private photo tokens are excluded; individual photo paths are grouped together. Privacy and administration pages are not counted. It does not keep individual event records, IP addresses, user-agent strings, referring sites, country, device information, visitor IDs or exact visit times. There are no profiles, session recordings, advertising, cross-site matching, or sales of analytics data. Aggregates cannot be linked back to a person, so I cannot retrieve or remove one person's contribution from a total.

Hosting and other site functions

Ordinary page delivery still requires hosting providers to process requests, which can include an IP address, requested URL and browser headers. Declining usage counts does not stop the requests needed to display the site. I use GitHub Pages for the personal site, and Vercel and Cloudflare for the photo site and counter. The photo site uses Cloudflare R2 to store photographs. Providers may process service and security logs under their own retention rules and international data-transfer arrangements. This is separate from the 90-day counter database; Western European database placement is not a promise that all requests or logs stay in the EU.

Necessary site delivery and security serve the legitimate interests of operating a reliable website and preventing abuse. Photo gallery administration uses a separate necessary sign-in cookie. The photo gallery is public: photographs and their sharing links are separate from optional usage counts. Please contact me to request removal of a photograph.

Your rights

Where personal data is processed, you may request access, correction, erasure or restriction, object to processing, and request portability where applicable. You may withdraw analytics consent using the controls below, and complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office or the data protection authority in your EU country. Email me for questions about processing or a rights request.
