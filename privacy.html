Simple website statistics

I count successful public page requests on the server to understand which content is useful. There is no analytics script, tracking cookie, local-storage identifier or browser fingerprint. The counter does not identify visitors, link visits into sessions, or match people between sites. No consent banner is needed for this implementation’s basic server statistics; this is not a claim that every form of server-side analytics is exempt from consent.

Each qualifying request contributes to separate daily totals for the site, public page, referring domain, country, broad device type and browser family. Country is approximate and provided by the hosting platform. Browser/device groups are inferred from ordinary request headers. Referrers are reduced to a domain, without paths, queries or subdomains. Query strings and private photo tokens are excluded. The counter does not save individual events, IP addresses, full browser headers, exact visit times or precise locations. Small country/device/browser groups with fewer than five page loads are hidden in reports.

Recognised bots, prefetch requests, administration and privacy pages are excluded. Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control request headers also exclude a request from statistics. These are estimates of page loads, not exact counts of people; cached browser restores and unrecognised bots can affect the totals. This processing serves my legitimate interest in maintaining and improving these personal sites with minimal information. You may object by contacting me.

Retention and hosting

Daily aggregates are kept for 90 days. Older site totals are rolled into monthly totals containing only the site, month and count, kept indefinitely for comparisons over many years. There are no visitor records to retrieve from those totals.

Vercel handles site delivery and derives the statistics in Frankfurt. Aggregates are sent through a Cloudflare Worker to an EU-jurisdiction Cloudflare D1 database. Vercel’s CDN and Cloudflare’s Worker gateway operate globally; this is best-effort European analytics processing and storage, not an EU-only guarantee for all infrastructure or provider logs. Hosting providers necessarily receive requests including IP addresses and headers for site delivery and security, under their own retention and international transfer arrangements. Cloudflare R2 stores the photographs. The photo administration area uses a necessary sign-in cookie.
