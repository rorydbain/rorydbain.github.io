Privacy Policy

Brae keeps your library private.

Brae helps you find and compress large photos and videos. It is designed so your photo library stays on your device.

Photos and videos

Brae asks for access to your photo library so it can show your media, estimate file sizes, create compressed versions, and save or replace items when you choose to. Your photos and videos are processed locally on your device and are not uploaded to Brae or to any server operated by me.

Data Brae stores

Brae may store small local app data on your device, such as compression history, thumbnails for completed batches, settings, purchase state, and stats such as space saved. This data is used to make the app work and is not sold or used for advertising.

Purchases

Paid features are handled through Apple. Apple may process purchase information according to its own privacy policy. Brae only uses purchase status to unlock features in the app.

Tracking and advertising

  • Brae does not track you across apps or websites.
  • Brae does not show third-party ads.
  • Brae does not sell personal data.

Support

If you contact me for support, I will use the information you send to understand and respond to your request. You can reach me at rorydbain@gmail.com.

Changes

I may update this policy as Brae changes. The latest version will always be available on this page.
