Privacy policy

Heart Rate Sync is designed to keep your workout data on your devices and under your control.

Heart rate sharing

During a workout, the app reads heart rate from Apple Health on your iPhone. When you tap Share Heart Rate, it sends the current reading over Bluetooth to receivers you choose to connect, such as a bike computer or training app. Anyone using a connected receiver can see that reading. What the receiver does with the data is governed by its own privacy practices.

Workout data

The app starts an indoor cycling workout session to collect live heart rate. By default, it saves the finished workout and collected heart rate to Apple Health on your iPhone. You can turn off Save workouts to Health in Settings before a session; the app then discards the workout when sharing stops. Recent readings are held in app memory for the graph and are cleared when you start another session or close the app. You can delete previously saved workouts in Fitness.

Optional features

If you enable Apple Music controls, the app reads the current song and controls playback on your iPhone. Connection and missing-reading alerts use local notifications.

Accounts, servers, and control

The app has no account, analytics, or app server, and its developer does not upload or retain your workout data. You can stop Bluetooth heart rate updates by tapping Stop Sharing. You can change Health, Bluetooth, Music, and notification access in iPhone Settings.
