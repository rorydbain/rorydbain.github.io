iPhone app support

Heart rate, wherever you ride.

Heart Rate Sync reads live workout heart rate on your iPhone and shares it with a compatible Bluetooth heart rate receiver, such as a bike computer or training app.

Get connected

  1. Wear AirPods Pro 3 or another compatible heart rate source.
  2. Open the app and tap Share Heart Rate.
  3. On your other device, add a Bluetooth heart rate sensor. Choose Heart Rate Sync or your iPhone’s name.

Pair while the iPhone is unlocked. The receiver must support Bluetooth Low Energy heart rate sensors; ANT+ is not transmitted.

If readings stop

Check the fit and settings of your heart rate source, and confirm it is providing readings on your iPhone. If your receiver still says connected but no heart rate arrives, disconnect and reconnect its Bluetooth heart rate sensor.

Keep the app open during initial pairing. When you stop sharing, the app saves the workout to Apple Health unless you turned off Save workouts to Health in Settings before starting.

Hammerhead Karoo

Keep Heart Rate Sync open and sharing while adding it as a Bluetooth heart rate sensor. Check for a changing BPM on a ride data page or ride preview. Keep the Hammerhead companion app paired for route syncing.

If your iPhone repeatedly asks to pair with Karoo BT Audio, open Settings → Bluetooth Audio → Pair new device on Karoo to find its confirmation. Approve only matching codes on both devices; contacts and call-history access is not needed.

If the app has readings but Karoo does not, reconnect its heart rate sensor. Keep the working sensor entry and remove older duplicates after checking which one receives readings.

Need help?

Tell us your iPhone model, iOS version, heart rate source, receiver model, and what you see on the ride screen. Please avoid sending personal health records.
